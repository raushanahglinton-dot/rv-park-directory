About this directory

Most RV park directories bury small, independently-run campgrounds under the biggest, most-booked resorts. We track both — side by side, filterable by size — so neither gets lost.

Why we track both sizes

Smaller parks (under 100 sites) tend to be quieter, more personal, and often better located than large RV resorts. They're also harder to find — big booking platforms rank them lower because they generate less revenue. Many have no website, no Campspot listing, and no social media. They exist in a database entry, a phone book, or word of mouth.

Larger resorts (100+ sites) matter too — they're what most travelers search for, and skipping them would make this a partial directory. Use the Site Count filter on the directory page to switch between "Under 100 sites" and "100+ sites," or search across both.

What's included

Every park in this directory is currently a privately owned or operated campground — family-run parks and full-size commercial resorts alike, ranging from paved full-service to gravel-and-fire-ring simple. Federal, State Park, and Corps of Engineers campgrounds may be added in the future.

Data sources

Park data is sourced from:

  • Recreation.gov (official federal campground database)
  • AllStays Camp & RV app (the most complete campground database in North America)
  • Campendium (user-reviewed, includes site counts in reviews)
  • RV Life Campground Reviews (campgrounds.rvlife.com)
  • The Dyrt (thedyrt.com)
  • State park reservation systems
  • Direct verification via phone and Google Maps Q&A

Accuracy

Site counts, hookup types, and phone numbers can change. Parks open, close, expand, or change ownership. We do our best to keep data current, but always call ahead or check the booking system before arriving. If you spot an error, the fix is simple — edit data.js and update the entry.

How to contribute

This site is built to grow. If you know an RV park — any size — that isn't listed, see the How to Add a State guide. The data file is plain JavaScript — no database, no backend, no account required.
